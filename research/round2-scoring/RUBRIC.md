# Scoring rubric (0-100), shared by all 10 evaluators

The founder's criteria are: proven market/demand, sellable (a company won't just do it themselves), survives AI progress (not obsolete or replaceable by Copilot/ChatGPT/Claude), solves a real evidence-backed problem, and a specific market to sell to. We add a feasibility/wedge criterion because the founders are a student team at the ideation stage that must ship an MVP fast.

| Criterion | Weight | What a max score requires |
|---|---|---|
| A. Proven demand / market | 20 | Quantified pain plus money already spent (budgets, competitors with revenue, acquisitions), and a growing trend |
| B. Sellable (won't build in-house; will pay) | 20 | Cross-system/cross-vendor complexity or neutrality that internal teams won't build; a clear budget line; credible pricing |
| C. AI-durability | 20 | The moat is data, integrations, workflow position or trust, NOT the LLM. Gets better, not obsolete, as models improve. Not a feature that GitHub, Atlassian, Anthropic or Cursor will ship next quarter |
| D. Evidence-backed real problem | 15 | Multiple independent sources (community quotes + surveys/studies), not only vendor claims |
| E. Specific, reachable buyer | 10 | A named title, company size and segment; a reachable channel for a student team |
| F. Feasibility & wedge for a student team | 15 | A credible MVP in weeks with public APIs; a narrow wedge that doesn't require an enterprise sales cycle or SOC2 on day 1; differentiated vs incumbents |

Be calibrated and harsh: 50 = mediocre, 70 = strong, 85+ = exceptional. Do NOT inflate. Penalize ideas that are crowded or that incumbents are already shipping.
