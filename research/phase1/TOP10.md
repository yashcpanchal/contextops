# Phase 1 synthesis: Top 10 candidate ideas

Synthesized by the orchestrator (Opus) from 5 research reports in this folder (reddit.md, x_hn_blogs.md, competitors.md, ai_era.md, visibility_people.md; reddit_hn_api.md if present). Date: 2026-10-13.

## Cross-source findings that shape the list
1. **AI shifted the bottleneck from writing code to reviewing, coordinating and justifying it.** Faros: PRs +98%, review time +91%, company-level outcomes flat. CMU/Stanford: PR volume 3.1x vs reviewers 1.5x. Reddit: "won't review AI PRs" (1,920 up), "I don't know, Claude wrote this" (854 up).
2. **Managers have lost the plot on what their team does.** Gallup spans went from 10.9 to 12.1 reports, with Meta teams at 50:1. Reddit: "engineering management memory crisis" (116 up), and EMs hand-roll Obsidian+AI and git/Jira scripts. This is the founder's Visa anecdote.
3. **Status reporting is the most-repeated time sink** ("the status updates became the actual work"). But Claude + Jira MCP is the stated DIY substitute, so a thin summarizer wrapper loses.
4. **AI spend became an unmanaged P&L line.** Gartner says AI coding cost will exceed developer salary by 2028. Uber burned its 2026 budget in about 4 months. DX found 86% of leaders unsure which tools pay off, and Reddit reports "tokenomics meetings" and $60k/mo doubling. Incumbents (Jellyfish, Faros, DX/Atlassian) are moving in.
5. **Surveillance backlash is the #1 adoption risk.** 54% of developers fear AI-based evaluation, and LinearB is seen as PIP ammunition. Team-level, IC-consented, developmental framing defuses it (Cornell).
6. **The original "ticket cost estimation" idea**: all 5 researchers independently judged it weak as a standalone product and promising as a derived feature once attribution telemetry exists. Reasons: no demand signal, estimation is low willingness-to-pay, calendar time is dominated by handoffs/review, and "employee efficiency" is toxic.
7. **The SEI dashboard market is consolidating** (DX to Atlassian for ~$1B, Graphite to Cursor). Acquirers validate demand, but the dashboards are commodities. White space: cross-vendor and agent-aware, action-taking (not dashboards), mid-market, trust-first.

## The 10 ideas
1. **AI Spend-to-Outcome Ledger ("FinOps for AI coding").** Cross-vendor (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, Codex) attribution of token and seat spend to team, repo, PR and ticket, joined to outcomes (merged, reverted, review time). Includes budgets, anomaly alerts, chargeback and policy guardrails. Buyer: VP Eng/CTO plus finance.
2. **True Cost of Delivery + calibrated forecasting (the original idea, reframed).** Retrospective per-ticket/feature cost = agent tokens + human review time + rework + wait time. Once history exists, it forecasts new tickets with honest ranges and recommends human vs agent routing. Team/ticket-type level only, no per-person efficiency. Buyer: VP Eng / Dir Eng, finance.
3. **Manager Memory / Team Context Engine.** A persistent, living per-person and per-project context built from GitHub, Jira/Linear, Slack, docs and agent sessions. It produces 1:1 prep briefs, "who's blocked or struggling", and project state, for EMs with 8-25+ reports. ICs can see what the manager sees. Buyer: Dir Eng (EM daily user).
4. **Evidence-linked status autopilot.** It drafts weekly leadership/stakeholder updates where every claim links to a PR, ticket or doc, with per-audience views (VP, PM, exec) and drift/risk flags. It replaces the Friday status ritual and the "chase 3 people on Slack" step. Buyer: EM/Dir Eng, PMO.
5. **Review-capacity orchestrator.** Measures reviewer load and review debt. It triages AI-generated PRs by risk, novelty and blast radius, routes them to the right reviewer, flags rubber-stamped merges, and auto-splits large PRs. Buyer: EM/Dir Eng, platform lead.
6. **AI-PR comprehension and provenance layer.** Captures the plan/agent session behind each diff, auto-generates a "why/how" explainer, runs lightweight author comprehension checks, and gives managers an AI-usage audit trail ("who understands what they shipped"). Buyer: EM/tech lead; security for audit.
7. **Agent governance and audit trail for regulated enterprises.** Records which agent, prompt, model, files touched and human approver for every change. Adds policy-as-code over what agents may touch (auth, payments, PII) and exportable evidence for SOX/PCI/model-risk audits. Beachhead: fintech/banks (Visa-like). Buyer: CISO/Head of Platform/Risk.
8. **Consent-based impact ledger to perf/promo evidence.** ICs get an auto-drafted weekly "brag doc" from real work (including glue work) that they approve. Managers get cited evidence packets for reviews, promo and calibration, which counters recency bias. Buyer: Head of Eng + HRBP; IC daily user.
9. **Flow & dependency risk radar.** Predicts calendar slippage from non-coding signals: cross-team handoffs, review queue age, approval waits, dependency blocks. It alerts the EM/TPM early and attributes delay to causes. Buyer: Dir/VP Eng, TPM org.
10. **Human+agent capacity planner and work router.** Learns from org history which ticket types agents complete cheaply vs blow up. It recommends assignment (agent / human / pair), enforces WIP limits on parallel agent streams, and plans sprint capacity across humans + agents. Buyer: EM/Dir Eng.

Ideas considered and folded in or dropped: AI readiness/ROI audit (a GTM motion for #1/#2), self-writing standup (crowded, folded into #4), intern/new-hire visibility copilot (founder-story fit but seasonal/weak buyer; folded into #3), AI adoption enablement analytics (folded into #1), telemetry normalization infra (an enabler for #1/#2).
