## Verified facts from round 3
- **Factory acquired Mentlio** (YC S26, the closest ContextOps look-alike) on 2026-10-09. This is confirmed on both Mentlio's YC page and Factory's news page. It reads as an acqui-hire into a coding-agent vendor ("Mentlio will bring this work into Factory"). This is evidence of absorption, and it also shows a fast acqui-hire exit path exists.
- **The YC AI-spend cohort has no traction.** About 8 companies were funded in W26/S26: Mentlio, Carrot Labs/SuperPenguin (Free/$30/$200 per month, verified), Quotient Labs ($20/month or 10% of savings), Conifer, Codag ($499/month team), Compresr, Orchestra and The Token Company. **None shows a named customer or ARR, and none has a finance export.**
- **Taxnova** (a16z Speedrun SR006) has real named customers (incident.io, causaLens, Augment; Paddle on its Speedrun page) for R&D-credit and CapEx evidence from Jira/GitHub. It does **not** cover AI token spend. Finance pays when there is a cash event, such as the credit.
- **The a16z post** (2026-08-27, "You are not a model. Don't price per token") tells AI *vendors* to ship spend tracing and chargeback themselves. That is an absorption signal, not an invitation for third-party tools (the round-3 synthesis initially misread it).
- **Faros shipped "AI Coding Cost Allocation"** with per-team finance statements (Sep 2026). Ramp launched AI spend categorization (Jul 2026). Ascerta (formerly Pay-i) raised an $18M Series A.
- **Buyers pay for evidence, review and outcomes**: cubic for AI code review (named customers), Mesmer for engineering outcomes, Taxnova for credit evidence. No cost dashboard shows paying customers.

## Implications for ContextOps (adopted in STRATEGY.md §15c)
1. **Keep VP Eng as the front door.** Finance-first scored lower (54) because the controller sale is slow and AI-token capitalization still has no buyer voice.
2. **Lead with the outcome join and evidence, not "cut your token bill."** Token-cost cutting is the most crowded, least-proven slice: 8 YC companies, 0 customers, and one already absorbed.
3. **Treat agent vendors as absorbers.** Factory/Mentlio, the a16z advice to vendors, and native caps in Copilot, Cursor and Claude all point the same way. The defensible position is the cross-vendor, vendor-neutral view joined to PRs and incidents (no single agent vendor will report on its competitors), plus evidence exports.
4. **Fold in two features from the round-3 losers:**
   - a cross-vendor agent-session log linked to PRs (from R3-4);
   - a finance evidence packet for customers that claim R&D credits or capitalize (from R3-1).
   Neither is the headline.
