Sources: DX survey of 275 leaders · Gartner, Jun 2026 · FinOps Foundation, State of FinOps 2026

Every engineering team is now paying for AI coding tools like Claude Code, Cursor and Copilot, and many pay for several at once. That bill is growing fast: Gartner expects AI coding costs to pass the average developer's salary by 2028. But 86% of engineering leaders say they can't tell which of these tools actually pays off. The person stuck in the middle is the engineering manager. They now own a budget that grows every month, and they have no way to connect that spend to the work that actually shipped. That's the problem we're solving.
