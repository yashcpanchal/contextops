This semester we went from an idea to a working product. First, we did the research: we read over 2,200 posts from engineers and managers, and scored 35 different startup ideas against the same rubric. This one won every round. Then we built it. Our MVP connects Claude Code, Cursor and Copilot, shows a team what it spends and what that spending actually shipped, and fixes waste like idle seats or runaway agents in one click, with approval. Next, we're talking to 20 engineering leaders and signing 5 design partners to prove they'll pay for it.
